Mullholt privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 13 November 2026
Applies to: Mullholt 0.1.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. Mullholt does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry or affiliate links. It makes no network requests and contains no remote code. Layouts and undo history stay on your device.

What is stored. In the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) on your device: saved layout names and relative column sizes. A most recently deleted layout may be kept so you can undo that deletion; the next save or deletion replaces it. In session storage, for up to five undo steps: tab identifiers, tab order, active and pinned state, and window bounds. This undo history is cleared when the browser session ends. Mullholt does not persist browsing URLs or page content in the undo history. This data is not sent to the publisher or to anyone else, and Mullholt does not sync it between devices.

Tab operations. The tabs permission lets Mullholt identify the current and next tabs and move them between windows, preserving their order. Page contents are never read or changed. Closing a page is not reversed by Undo split. Any local start pages are bundled with the extension. No website access is requested. The browser controls private-window access; the extension is designed for normal desktop browser windows.

Network. Mullholt makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. Its own pages block network connections through a content security policy (connect-src 'none'). The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual. Mullholt does not add to, read or change that traffic. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. If you open a legal or support link in your browser, the hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request records; no extension or layout data is attached.

Permissions. - tabs: identify, move and restore the tabs in a split. Page contents are never read or changed; - storage: keep saved layouts on this device and a short undo history for the current session. No host permissions and no automatic content scripts are declared at install.

Deletion. You can delete layouts in Settings. Uninstalling Mullholt deletes everything it stored in the browser.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights. Where GDPR applies, you may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights, and complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Mullholt does not send extension data to the publisher, so most requests concern only messages you choose to send by email.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
