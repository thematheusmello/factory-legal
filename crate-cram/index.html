Crate Cram

Crate Cram is a calm block-placement puzzle with a logistics twist. Drag crate pieces onto an 8×8 warehouse floor and fill rows or columns to clear them. Clear lines with matching shipping stamps for Express bonuses that load your delivery truck; a full truck earns a forklift that clears any 3×3 area. No timer, five warehouse themes, and your run is saved so you can continue later. Free to play on supported game platforms (for example CrazyGames and YouTube Playables), with ads provided by the platform.
