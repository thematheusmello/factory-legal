Lintelholt privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 13 November 2026
Applies to: Lintelholt 0.1.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. Lintelholt does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry, advertising, remote rules or affiliate links. It makes no network requests of its own and contains no remote code. Profiles, header values, scopes and the master switch stay on your device.

What is stored. In the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) on your device: profiles, header values, scopes and the master switch. This data stays on the device and is not synced by Lintelholt. Tab-only bindings use session storage and expire when the tab or browser session ends. Local storage is not encrypted: avoid storing long-lived credentials in header values, and protect any exported files yourself. This data is not sent to the publisher or to anyone else.

Header rules and authorized sites. Enabled rules change headers on requests to the sites you authorize. Those sites receive the headers you configure, including any identifiers or credentials you put in them, under their own policies. Response rules change headers delivered by those sites to your browser. Use only on sites and systems you may test. Host access is optional and is requested when you add a scope. You may revoke it in Firefox’s extension permissions. Revocation stops rules for that host.

Network. Lintelholt makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. Its own pages block network connections through a content security policy (connect-src 'none'). The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual. When a profile and the master switch are on, Firefox’s declarative request engine applies your header rules to authorized traffic; Lintelholt does not otherwise add to, read or change that traffic, and it does not redirect URLs, block requests, rewrite bodies or inject scripts. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. Export writes a JSON file only when you choose Export. Importing a file reads it on the device and leaves all imported profiles disabled. Legal and support links open only when you click them; if you open one in your browser, the hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request records; no extension or profile data is attached.

Permissions. - storage: saved local profiles and the master switch; session tab bindings; - declarativeNetRequest: modify request and response headers on authorized sites; - activeTab: current-page preview and tab-only scope from the toolbar action; - optional website access: requested for the host or pattern you add. No website access is granted on a fresh installation. Path scopes remain enforced by the rules even though Firefox’s permission grant covers the whole host.

Deletion. Delete profiles in the editor, or uninstall Lintelholt, to remove extension data. Remove exported files wherever you saved them.

Support emails. Emailing support shares what you send with the publisher and email providers. No support message is sent automatically. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights. Where GDPR applies, you may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights, and complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Lintelholt does not send extension data to the publisher, so most requests concern only messages you choose to send by email.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
