DraftPebble

DraftPebble is a free Firefox desktop add-on that gives unfinished words a soft landing. On websites you choose, it saves text as you type in ordinary text fields, so an accidental reload or a closed tab does not have to mean starting again.

  • Open DraftPebble from the toolbar and choose Protect this site. Firefox then asks whether to allow access to that site. Nothing is saved before you agree.
  • Type as usual. On protected pages the toolbar button shows how many drafts that exact page can recover.
  • Come back to the same page, open DraftPebble and choose Restore to empty field. Restoring never replaces text that is already in a field. Review restored text before you submit it.
  • Pause saving, delete one draft, delete all saved text after a confirmation step, stop protecting a site, or remove site access. Each draft expires seven days after its last captured edit.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links and no payments.

Privacy. Drafts and settings stay in this Firefox profile, in the add-on's local storage. They are not encrypted by DraftPebble and are never synced or uploaded. DraftPebble makes no network requests of its own. Websites still make their own network requests while you browse, and their scripts can read text you restore, just as they can read text you type. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale support is claimed. Passwords, recognised payment and security fields, fields marked autocomplete off, read-only and disabled fields are skipped, but detection relies on field types and labels and cannot recognise every private value, so only protect sites where saving what you type is appropriate. Rich-text editors, embedded frames, fields inside shadow DOM, non-text inputs, browser and add-on store pages and some custom forms are not supported. Up to 200 fields, 20,000 characters per field and 500,000 characters in total. Saving is best effort, not a backup. No keyboard shortcuts.
