Prosewake privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 12 November 2026
Applies to: Prosewake 1.0.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 140 or later).

Summary. Prosewake does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry, affiliate links or cloud speech. Article and selection text is processed only on your device, and speech uses only local device voices.

What is stored. In the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) on your device: your voice, rate, pitch and highlight palette; whether Prosewake is enabled; and optional per-site reading speeds. This data is not sent to the publisher or to anyone else, and Prosewake does not sync it between devices.

Page content. When you choose Read this page, Read selection, or the selection context menu, Prosewake extracts text from the top document in your browser, speaks it with a local voice, and highlights the current sentence in the reading player and on the page. Temporary highlight marks are removed when you stop, leave, or move to the next sentence. Page text is not stored after you stop and is not transmitted. Selection-only reading does not keep a copy of the selection beyond the current session.

Network. Prosewake makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. Its own pages block network connections through a content security policy (connect-src 'none'). The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual. Prosewake does not add to, read or change that traffic. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. If you open a legal or support link in your browser, the hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request records; no extension or reading data is attached.

Permissions. - activeTab: access the current top document only after you invoke Prosewake; - scripting: inject extraction and the reading player after that action; - storage: save preferences and site speeds on your device; - contextMenus: add “Read selection with Prosewake” to the selection menu. Firefox uses the window speechSynthesis API and does not request a tts permission. No host permissions and no automatic content scripts are declared at install.

Deletion. You can reset site speeds or change preferences in settings. Uninstalling Prosewake deletes everything it stored in the browser.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights. Where GDPR applies, you may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights, and complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Prosewake does not send extension data to the publisher, so most requests concern only messages you choose to send by email.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
