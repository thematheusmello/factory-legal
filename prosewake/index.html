Prosewake

Prosewake is a free Firefox desktop add-on that reads the current article or your selected text aloud, using voices already on your device, with a clear sentence highlight to help you follow along. Everything stays on your device.

  • Open the toolbar button on an article and choose Read this page. Or select a passage and choose Read selection, including from the context menu. A collapsed selection falls back to the article.
  • Pause, resume, skip a sentence, or click a paragraph in the article or the reading player to begin there. The current sentence is highlighted both in the player and on the page, and the page scrolls it into view.
  • Choose a comfortable voice, speed, pitch and highlight palette (cream with blue or cream with amber, both outlined in high contrast). Your speed can be remembered for each site on this device.
  • Large controls and keyboard shortcuts keep reading within reach. Ctrl+Shift+U reads the page; Ctrl+Shift+Y reads the selection. F8 pauses or resumes, and Esc stops and removes the player. Shortcuts can be remapped in Firefox and may conflict with other shortcuts.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links, no payments and no cloud speech.

Privacy. Speech uses only local device voices. Page text is extracted and spoken on your device and is not sent to the publisher or to a speech service. Preferences stay in local extension storage and are never synced. Prosewake itself makes no network requests and uses connect-src 'none'. Websites you visit still make their own traffic; Prosewake does not block or change that. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 140 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale support is claimed in this Firefox listing. Only the top document is supported. PDFs, frames, browser or system pages, and cloud voices are not supported. Some pages extract poorly. Very long passages without sentence punctuation are read in pieces of up to about 1,000 characters. Voice quality, language, pitch and pause behaviour depend on installed voice packs; install a local voice if none are available. The player is fixed at the bottom-right. Firefox Speech Dispatcher may expose thousands of voice variants; settings show one representative per language. On Firefox, Pause stops device speech and Resume repeats the current sentence from the beginning.
