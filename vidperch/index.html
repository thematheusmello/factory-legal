VidPerch – Video Picture-in-Picture

VidPerch is a free extension for Firefox desktop that floats web videos in your browser's native Picture-in-Picture window, so you can keep watching while you work in other tabs.

  • Click the VidPerch toolbar button, press the suggested Ctrl+Shift+8 shortcut, or right-click and choose Float video with VidPerch.
  • If the page has several videos, a numbered picker lets you choose one.
  • If the browser needs one more click, press Float this video on the page.
  • Playback is handled by your browser's own Picture-in-Picture controls.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Privacy. VidPerch works entirely in your browser. It makes no network requests and sends nothing to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy.
