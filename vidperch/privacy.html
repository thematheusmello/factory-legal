VidPerch privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 8 November 2026
Applies to: VidPerch 1.0.1 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 153 or later).

Summary. VidPerch opens a video you choose in your browser's native Picture-in-Picture window. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, telemetry or remote code, and it makes no network requests. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

What VidPerch accesses. When you click the toolbar button, use the shortcut or choose the context-menu item, VidPerch gets temporary access to the current tab (activeTab) and runs a small script there to find videos and show the numbered picker or the Float this video button. A video's source address may be used briefly to identify the video you picked; it is not stored or sent anywhere. VidPerch does not download, record or transmit videos, page titles, addresses or browsing history. Videos keep loading from the website as usual, under that site's own privacy policy.

Storage. VidPerch 1.0.1 stores nothing new. If you used version 1.0.0, older saved playback-speed preferences may remain in local extension storage. VidPerch reads them only to offer the Clear legacy saved speeds button in its popup, and they are also removed when you uninstall. Nothing is synchronised and no server is used.

Permissions. activeTab (temporary access to the tab you invoke VidPerch on), scripting (to run the video finder in that tab), storage (to read and clear legacy 1.0.0 preferences) and contextMenus (the Float video with VidPerch menu item). VidPerch has no host permissions and no always-on content scripts.

Privacy policy link. The privacy link in the popup opens this page only when you click it.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
