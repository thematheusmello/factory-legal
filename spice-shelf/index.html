Spice Shelf Sort

Spice Shelf Sort is a calm sorting puzzle set in a busy spice market. Drag jars between shelves and line up three of the same spice to seal and clear them; hidden jars wait behind the front row, so plan ahead and keep space free. Customers hang their orders above the shelves: clear the spices they ask for to earn bonus coins and decorate your stall with new shelf themes. No timer, 120 procedurally generated levels with up to three stars each, undo and hints when you need them, and your progress is saved so you can continue later. Free to play on supported game platforms (for example CrazyGames and YouTube Playables), with ads provided by the platform.
