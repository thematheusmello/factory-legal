Cobble Court privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Cobble Court is an HTML5 sports game published by Matheus da Silveira Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal (contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com). The game is played on third-party game platforms (“platforms”), such as CrazyGames and YouTube Playables, and as a local/itch build.

Summary

  • The game does not ask for a name, email, birthday or game account and does not assign a tracking identifier. There are no purchases.
  • The game has no analytics, game server or advertising requests. Ads are disabled.
  • Your progress is saved on your device or through the platform’s save feature.
  • Sharing a challenge is voluntary; the code contains only a seed and numeric score.

Save data

To let you continue, the game saves a small versioned JSON record: match positions and scores, CPU difficulty, deterministic random state, shot statistics, best performance score, series state, daily challenge record, and music and sound-effect on/off and volume settings. The local build uses browser localStorage under cobble-court. The CrazyGames build uses its SDK Data module. The YouTube Playables build uses host SDK storage and submits the same integer personal best kept in the save; outside the actual Playables host, its adapter may use local browser storage. The save contains no name, contact details or account identifier and is not sent to the publisher.

Browser-local saves remain until you or your browser clear the game’s site data. Clear site storage in your browser settings to remove the local match and statistics. Platform saves follow the platform’s storage and account controls. The game cannot access or delete the platform’s account data; contact the platform for those controls.

Challenges

An explicit challenge action shows a seed and numeric performance score as a code or link. Sharing is voluntary and performed by you. No name, account ID or personal statistics are included. Anyone with the code can replay the seed and see the target score; scores are not authenticated. Local and CrazyGames builds write to the clipboard only after you tap Copy link. The game never reads the clipboard. YouTube Playables uses selectable text, with no clipboard API or external challenge link.

The daily challenge derives its seed from your device’s UTC calendar date. No location or time service is contacted.

Platforms and ads

Only the selected portal SDK is loaded remotely; local play uses bundled resources only. Art and audio are generated locally. The game makes no advertising requests and its ad integration is disabled. Platforms and browsers may independently process account, device, connection and platform-storage information under their own privacy policies — for example CrazyGames and Google/YouTube. This policy does not describe or override their independent processing.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, push notifications, sign-in or payment permissions. Web Audio synthesizes music and sound effects locally after interaction, respecting your audio preferences and host audio restrictions. Clipboard writing is limited to the player-initiated Copy link action described above.

Children

Cobble Court is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

If you email me, I use your email only to answer you and keep the conversation only as long as needed. You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data I hold by writing to matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or the authority in your country.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
