Cobble Court

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Cobble Court is an HTML5 sports game published by Matheus da Silveira Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal. Play 1v1 street basketball against a CPU rival, first to eleven, on a cobbled courtyard rendered with Canvas 2D. The game is available on third-party platforms such as CrazyGames and YouTube Playables, and as a local/itch build.

  • Find space, time your shot and beat a CPU rival to eleven.
  • Continue a saved match, choose CPU difficulty and track shot statistics and your best performance score.
  • Play a daily challenge based on your device’s UTC date, or voluntarily share a challenge seed and target score.
  • Adjust music and sound effects, synthesized locally with Web Audio.

No game account, no analytics, no purchases and no ads requested by the game; ads are disabled.

Privacy. A small JSON save keeps your match, scores, difficulty, statistics, daily challenge record and audio settings in browser local storage or the platform’s save service. A voluntary challenge code contains only a seed and numeric score. The game never reads the clipboard. See the privacy policy.

Platforms. Third-party platforms process data under their own policies. YouTube Playables also receives the saved integer personal-best score. Browser-local saves remain until you or your browser clear the game’s site data; platform saves follow the platform’s controls.
