RecLocal – Screen Recorder, No Watermark

RecLocal is a free Firefox desktop add-on that records a screen or window you choose, saves the file on your device as WebM, and never uploads it.

  • Pick a screen or window in Firefox's own sharing prompt, then record after a 3-second countdown.
  • Optionally add your microphone or a webcam bubble. Both are off until you turn them on, and Firefox asks permission first.
  • Pause, resume and stop from the toolbar, the recorder tab or Ctrl+Shift+8 (tested on Firefox on Linux).
  • Preview, download as WebM, trim locally, and recover the last saved chunks if the browser or recorder tab closes unexpectedly.

Every feature is free. No watermark, no account, no uploads.

Firefox limits. Firefox cannot record tab or system sound. Direct tab capture is not available — choose a window or screen. MP4 export is not available; downloads are WebM. Keep the RecLocal recorder tab open while recording. Shortcut behaviour was tested on Firefox on Linux only.

Privacy. Recording, storage and editing stay on your device. RecLocal makes no network requests and sends nothing to us. See the privacy policy.
