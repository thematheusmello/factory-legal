FocusGate – Site Blocker & Schedules

FocusGate is a free site blocker extension for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Make room for what matters. FocusGate blocks the websites that pull your attention away, using your own rules and your device's clock.

  • Add domains or sections such as youtube.com/shorts, or start from Social, News, Video and Shopping presets.
  • Block always, or during multiple weekly windows, including overnight windows.
  • Start a 25, 50 or 90-minute focus session from the toolbar.
  • Use allow-list mode to block every website except the ones you choose.
  • Put your own reminder on the calm blocked page.
  • Add friction with lock mode: changing settings during a block takes an exact 60-character phrase and a 60-second pause.
  • See how often the calm page opened each day over 7 or 30 days.

Every feature is free, with no account or purchase. FocusGate has no site cap; Firefox's own rule and storage limits apply.

Your lists, schedules, reminder and daily counts are saved in Firefox's extension storage on your device. FocusGate makes no network requests and runs no scripts on websites. It keeps no list of pages you visit; the daily counts record how often the calm page opened, not which sites or when.

Site access is optional. When you add a site, Firefox asks whether FocusGate may access it. That access is used only to show the calm page. If you decline, the site is still blocked with Firefox's own error page, and those visits are not counted. "Block this site" reads the current tab's address only when you click it, and keeps just the site name.

Blocking applies to new top-level web page loads. Pages that are already open, Firefox's internal pages and protected Mozilla sites are outside its reach. Private windows need your permission. Schedules can run late after sleep or while Firefox is closed. Lock mode cannot stop you from disabling or removing an add-on.
