UnitWise privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 9 November 2026
Applies to: UnitWise – Unit Price Sorter for Amazon 0.2.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. UnitWise does not collect, sell or share personal data. Processing happens locally on Amazon search pages. It makes no network requests of its own, has no telemetry, and sends nothing to the publisher or anyone else. What it stores stays in your browser on your device.

What is read. UnitWise runs only on Amazon search result pages: www.amazon.com, www.amazon.co.uk, www.amazon.ca, www.amazon.de, www.amazon.fr, www.amazon.it and www.amazon.es, on addresses whose path starts with /s? or /s/. On those pages it reads, inside your browser: product titles, displayed prices and unit-price labels; sponsored-result markers (when that option is on); and the search term in the page address (only to remember an “items per use” value you set for that search). It does not run on product pages, the basket, checkout, your account or any other website, and it does not read cookies, passwords, order history or payment details.

What is stored. In the browser’s local extension storage on this device: your settings (enabled, display units, auto-sort, hide sponsored, outline best values) and “items per use” values saved with their search term. No usage counters, identifiers or browsing history. No sync between devices.

Network. UnitWise itself sends no network requests to the publisher or any third party (extension pages use CSP connect-src 'none'). There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, affiliate links or developer servers. Amazon’s pages still load from Amazon as they normally do; that traffic is between your browser and Amazon and is covered by Amazon’s own privacy notice. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"].

Permissions. storage only (settings and per-search “items per use”). No host permissions beyond the content-script match patterns limited to those Amazon search pages. No commands / keyboard shortcuts.

Deletion. Clear saved search terms in UnitWise settings. Turn UnitWise off in settings. Removing the extension deletes all data it stored.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
