UnitWise – Unit Price Sorter for Amazon

UnitWise is a free Firefox desktop add-on that shows unit prices on Amazon search result pages and can sort the current page by them — local settings, no telemetry.

  • Adds a badge on each result: ✓ when the store already shows a unit price, ~ when UnitWise estimates it from the title, or “no unit price found”.
  • Sorts the current results page by unit price (lowest first). “Original order” puts the page back.
  • Optional: hide sponsored results, outline the three best values, hide items without a unit price, and auto-sort on load.
  • “Items per use” for count products, remembered per search term. Regional units (oz / fl oz on amazon.com; 100 g / litre elsewhere).

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Privacy. Processing happens locally on Amazon search pages. Settings stay in local extension storage. UnitWise sends nothing to us. Amazon’s own page traffic is the website’s. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Unit prices are informational estimates and may be inaccurate — they can miss sizes, multipacks, coupons, subscriptions, delivery costs or other discounts. Check the product listing before buying. Sorting reorders the current page only. Runs only on search pages (/s? or /s/) of www.amazon.com, .co.uk, .ca, .de, .fr, .it and .es. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale claim. No keyboard shortcut is declared.
