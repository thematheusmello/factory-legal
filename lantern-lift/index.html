Lantern Lift

Lantern Lift is a relaxing number-merge puzzle that runs upside down. Launch numbered paper lanterns into five sky columns, where they rise and stack; when equal lanterns touch, they merge into one brighter lantern of double the value. Plan chains, use your wind swaps wisely, and set new records: each big record lights a festival that releases your dimmest lanterns and opens up space. Endless play, five lantern styles to unlock, and your run is saved so you can continue later. Free to play on supported game platforms (for example CrazyGames and YouTube Playables), with ads provided by the platform.
