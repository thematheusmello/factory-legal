Plank Parlor privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Plank Parlor is an HTML5 puzzle game published by Matheus da Silveira Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal (contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com). The game is played on third-party game platforms (“platforms”), such as CrazyGames and YouTube Playables, and as a local build.

Summary

  • The game does not ask for a name, email, birthday or game account and does not assign a tracking identifier. There are no purchases.
  • The game has no analytics, game server or advertising requests. Ads are disabled.
  • Your progress is saved on your device or through the platform’s save feature.
  • Sharing a challenge is voluntary; the link contains only a seed, board type and numeric score.

Save data

To let you continue, the game saves a small versioned JSON record: best score, latest daily commission date and best, parquet trophy, music and sound-effect on/off and volume settings, and any unfinished run (its seed, mode, board, tray of pieces, score, clear streak, line and placement counts, parquet, remaining free undos, one-time reshuffle flag, deterministic random state and up to three earlier board states for undo). The local build uses browser localStorage under plank-parlor. The CrazyGames build uses its SDK Data module. The YouTube Playables build uses host SDK storage and submits the same integer personal best kept in the save; outside the actual Playables host, its adapter may use local browser storage. The save contains no name, contact details or account identifier and is not sent to the publisher.

Browser-local saves remain until you or your browser clear the game’s site data. Clear site storage in your browser settings to remove the local run, scores and trophy. Platform saves follow the platform’s storage and account controls. The game cannot access or delete the platform’s account data; contact the platform for those controls.

Challenges

On the local and CrazyGames builds, an explicit Challenge a friend action after a run shows a link with the starting seed, board type and numeric score to beat. Sharing is voluntary and performed by you. No name, account ID or personal statistics are included. Anyone with the link can replay the board and see the target score; scores are not authenticated. Select link only highlights the text; you copy it yourself with your device’s usual controls. The game never reads or writes the clipboard. Opened links are validated and are not stored as a separate record. The YouTube Playables build has no challenge link or sharing controls.

The daily commission derives its seed from your device’s UTC calendar date. No location or time service is contacted.

Platforms and ads

Only the selected portal SDK is loaded remotely; local play uses bundled resources only. Art and audio are generated locally and text uses your device’s fonts. The game makes no advertising requests and its ad integration is disabled. A labelled local developer test can simulate the optional tray reshuffle reward without contacting any ad service. The YouTube Playables build may tell the host that an error or warning occurred, with no details attached. Platforms and browsers may independently process account, device, connection and platform-storage information under their own privacy policies — for example CrazyGames and Google/YouTube. This policy does not describe or override their independent processing.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, push notifications, sign-in or payment permissions. Web Audio synthesizes music and sound effects locally after interaction, respecting your audio preferences and host audio restrictions. The game uses no clipboard access; you copy a challenge link yourself.

Children

Plank Parlor is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

If you email me, I use your email only to answer you and keep the conversation only as long as needed. You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data I hold by writing to matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or the authority in your country.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
