Typeflint privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 10 November 2026
Applies to: Typeflint 0.2.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. Typeflint is a free typography inspection add-on for desktop Firefox. It shows which typeface a page actually rendered and that page's typography values. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry or affiliate links, and the publisher runs no server for it. It does not send page text, typography values or anything else to the publisher or to anyone else, and it does not sell or share data. The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual; Typeflint does not make browsing network-free.

When it runs. Nothing runs in a page until you open the Typeflint toolbar popup and choose Inspect this page. Inspection lasts until you press Escape, choose Close or leave the page, and applies only to that tab.

What Typeflint reads. While inspecting, Typeflint reads, in that tab only:

  • the text of the element you hover, pin or step to, and, for the page summary, of the visible text-bearing elements;
  • the computed typography of those elements (font stack, size, weight, style, line height, letter spacing, colour) and the background colours behind them;
  • the page's font loading state (which web fonts are loaded) and glyph measurements, to work out which face was actually rendered.

These values stay in the tab's memory and appear only in the Typeflint panel. They are never stored, never sent to the publisher and never shared with anyone. Escape or Close removes the panel and every listener; leaving the page discards everything.

What Typeflint stores. One setting, in the browser's local extension storage (storage.local) for this browser profile: whether to show a short guide when inspection starts. It is never synced and is never uploaded. Uninstalling Typeflint removes it. No page text, addresses, history, font lists or copied CSS are stored.

Clipboard. When you click Copy CSS, Typeflint writes that CSS block to your clipboard. It never reads the clipboard. How long clipboard content is kept depends on your operating system and any clipboard manager you use.

What Typeflint does not do. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, affiliate links, payments, font lookups or remote code, and no data is sold or shared. Typeflint itself makes no network requests, never asks a page to download a font, and its pages enforce a content security policy that blocks network connections (connect-src 'none'). Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. This declaration does not mean browsing is network-free.

Websites' own traffic. Typeflint does not stop websites from making their own network requests (including their fonts, analytics and advertising) and is not a privacy shield. Firefox and the add-on store may also contact their own services, for example for updates, under their own policies.

Permissions. activeTab grants access to the tab you are on when you click the toolbar button. scripting adds the inspector to that tab when you choose Inspect this page. storage keeps the guide setting described above. Typeflint has no host permissions, defines no keyboard shortcuts, and cannot run on browser pages or add-on stores.

Children. Typeflint is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights. Typeflint does not collect personal data, so there is nothing to access, export, correct or delete beyond uninstalling the add-on (which removes the guide setting).

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months. What you send is handled separately from the extension.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the notice will be published at https://thematheusmello.github.io/factory-legal/font-finder/.
