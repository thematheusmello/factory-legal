PicHarvest – Bulk Image Downloader

PicHarvest is a free Firefox desktop add-on that finds images on the page you’re viewing, lets you filter and select them, then save originals, one ZIP, or PNG/JPG converted from WebP/AVIF — settings stay local.

  • Click the PicHarvest button, then Scan page. It finds regular images, srcset and picture sources, CSS backgrounds, lazy-load attributes, inline SVG and links to image files.
  • Filter by minimum size, type, text in the image address, or same-site only; select all, none or individuals.
  • Download selected originals through Firefox’s downloads; Download ZIP (up to 1,000 images / 200 MB uncompressed); convert WebP/AVIF to PNG or JPG on your device.
  • Choose a download folder and filename template with a live example.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no tracking, no usage limits.

Privacy. Settings stay in local extension storage. When you download, make a ZIP or convert, PicHarvest requests the image files directly from the websites hosting them — those sites see a normal request. Nothing goes to us. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Protected browser and add-on store pages, unreachable frames, login-gated or refused images, and script-drawn pictures may not be found or saved. Saving an image doesn’t grant rights to use it. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale claim. No keyboard shortcut is declared.
