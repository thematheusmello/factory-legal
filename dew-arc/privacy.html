Dewlit Arc privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Dewlit Arc is an HTML5 aim-and-pop bubble shooter published by Matheus da Silveira Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal (contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com). The game is played on third-party game platforms (“platforms”), such as CrazyGames and YouTube Playables, and as a local build.

Summary

  • The game does not ask for a name, email, birthday or game account and does not assign a tracking identifier. There are no purchases.
  • The game has no analytics, game server or advertising requests. Live ads are disabled.
  • Your progress is saved on your device or through the platform’s save feature.
  • Sharing a challenge is voluntary; the link contains only a seed, claimed score, shot count, garden type and level.

Save data

To let you continue, the game saves a small versioned JSON record: best score, unlocked garden level, completion and tutorial flags, lifetime counts of cleared gardens, shots, popped and dropped beads, latest daily date, score, shots and win status, music and sound-effect on/off and volume settings, and the current run (seed, deterministic random state, mode, level, board, current and next bead, bead flight, score, shots, elapsed time, mist descent, miss pressure, combo, breeze, play/win/loss status, bead-swap flag and any challenge target). The local build uses browser localStorage under dew-arc. The CrazyGames build uses its SDK Data module under the same key. The YouTube Playables build uses host SDK storage and submits the same integer personal best kept in the save after saving, when the host is not paused; outside the actual Playables host, its adapter may use local browser storage. The save contains no name, contact details or account identifier and is not sent to the publisher.

Browser-local saves remain until you or your browser clear the game’s site data. Clear site storage in your browser settings to remove the local run, scores, progression and settings. Platform saves follow the platform’s storage and account controls. The game cannot access or delete the platform’s account data; contact the platform for those controls.

Challenges

On the local and CrazyGames builds, an explicit challenge action after a run shows selectable text with a link containing the starting seed, claimed score, shot count, garden type and level in its URL fragment. Sharing is voluntary and performed by you. No name or account ID is included. Anyone with the link can replay the garden and see the target score and shots; these claims are not authenticated. Select text only highlights the text; you copy it yourself with your device’s usual controls. The game never reads or writes the clipboard and does not use Web Share. Incoming challenge fragments are validated and processed locally; any target used in a run is included in that run’s save. The YouTube Playables build accepts incoming challenge seeds but has no sharing controls or clipboard access.

The daily garden derives its seed from your device’s UTC calendar date. No location or time service is contacted.

Platforms and ads

Only the selected portal SDK is loaded remotely; local play uses bundled resources only. Art and audio are generated locally and text uses your device’s fonts. The game makes no advertising requests; live ads are disabled. The optional one-use bead swap per garden is a free test reward and does not contact an ad service. The YouTube Playables build may tell the host that an error or warning occurred, with no details attached. Platforms and browsers may independently process account, device, connection and platform-storage information under their own privacy policies — for example CrazyGames and Google/YouTube. This policy does not describe or override their independent processing.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, push notifications, sign-in or payment permissions. Web Audio synthesizes music and sound effects locally after interaction, respecting your audio preferences and host pause and audio restrictions; it does not record audio. The game uses no clipboard or Web Share access; you copy challenge text yourself on supported builds.

Children

Dewlit Arc is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

If you email me, I use your email only to answer you and keep the conversation only as long as needed. You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data I hold by writing to matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or the authority in your country.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
