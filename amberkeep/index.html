Amberkeep

Amberkeep is a free Firefox desktop add-on that lets you inspect, edit, import, export and snapshot cookies for the site you are on. Changes stay on your device. Site access is requested only when you choose to grant it.

  • Open the toolbar button (Alt+Shift+K) on a website and review that site’s cookies in a searchable table. Flags such as Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite are easy to see. A Firefox sidebar is available when you want a wider, persistent workspace.
  • Inspect or edit a cookie’s name, value, domain, path, expiry and flags. Create, delete, or delete all after a confirmation. Changing a cookie’s identity writes the new cookie and then removes the old one.
  • Save named snapshots of the current site’s cookies and restore one later. Import and export Netscape cookie text or JSON, all processed on your device.
  • Turn on read-only mode to block set, remove, import and restore across open Amberkeep surfaces, while inspection, export and snapshot saving remain available.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links and no payments. Amberkeep does not send cookie values, snapshots or your preferences anywhere.

Privacy. Cookie work happens through the browser’s cookie APIs on your device. Named snapshots and your read-only preference stay in local extension storage and are never synced. Amberkeep itself makes no network requests and uses connect-src 'none'. Websites you visit still make their own traffic and may continue to set cookies independently. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 140 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale support is claimed in this Firefox listing. Internal browser pages cannot be managed. A sidebar opened directly may lack access until you invoke the toolbar on the desired site. In the Firefox toolbar popup, Esc closes the entire popup even if an editor or confirmation is open; unsaved drafts are discarded and pending confirmations are cancelled, not applied. Use the Firefox sidebar if you need a workspace that stays open.
