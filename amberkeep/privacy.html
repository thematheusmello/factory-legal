Amberkeep privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 12 November 2026
Applies to: Amberkeep 1.0.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 140 or later).

Summary. Amberkeep does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry or affiliate links. Cookie inspection, editing, import, export and snapshots stay on your device. Amberkeep does not send cookie values or snapshots to the publisher or to anyone else.

What is stored. In the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) on your device: named cookie snapshots you choose to save, and your read-only preference. This data is not sent to the publisher or to anyone else, and Amberkeep does not sync it between devices. Cookie records themselves live in the browser’s site cookie store and are changed only when you request an action.

Cookie operations. After you grant site access, Amberkeep can list, create, update or remove cookies for that site through the browser’s cookie APIs. Edits, imports, exports, restores and deletions happen on your device. Exported files are saved where you choose and are not uploaded. Imported files are read on your device. A website may continue to set cookies independently of Amberkeep.

Network. Amberkeep makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. Its own pages block network connections through a content security policy (connect-src 'none'). The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual. Amberkeep does not add to, read or change that traffic. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. If you open a legal or support link in your browser, the hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request records; no extension or cookie data is attached.

Permissions. - cookies: read and write cookies for sites you have granted access to; - activeTab: work with the site you invoke from the toolbar; - storage: save snapshots and the read-only preference on your device; - optional host access (<all_urls>): requested only when you choose Allow site access. Amberkeep then asks for the current scheme and host, not a silent grant for every website. You can revoke access in Firefox’s add-on settings. Default shortcut: Alt+Shift+K (changeable in Firefox). A sidebar opened directly may lack access until you invoke the toolbar on the desired site.

Deletion. You can delete snapshots in Amberkeep, revoke site permission in browser settings, remove downloaded files, or uninstall the extension to remove its local data. Cookies remain in the browser’s site cookie store unless you delete them there or through Amberkeep.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights. Where GDPR applies, you may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights, and complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Amberkeep does not send extension data to the publisher, so most requests concern only messages you choose to send by email.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
