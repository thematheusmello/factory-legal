StyleSnap privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how StyleSnap – Hover CSS Inspector & Copier (“StyleSnap”), a browser extension for Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox published by Matheus Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal, handles information. Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com.

Local use and page access

StyleSnap is free and requires no account. It runs its bundled inspector in the selected tab when you click the toolbar button or use its keyboard shortcut. It does not automatically inspect other tabs. Browser and store pages may block access, and content inside embedded frames is not inspected.

While open, the inspector reads selected elements’ tag names, IDs, classes, dimensions and computed CSS. “With children” also reads descendants to produce CSS and a minimal HTML skeleton containing tag names, IDs and classes, without page text or other attributes. “:hover styles” reads accessible stylesheet rules and skips inaccessible sheets. CSS values themselves can contain URLs or other sensitive page information.

The inspector handles mouse and keyboard events to select elements and respond to shortcuts; it does not record typed input. It inserts a panel and highlight overlays into the document and temporarily inserts baseline elements to compare browser defaults. Page scripts may observe these document changes. Esc, closing the panel, clicking the toolbar button again, or leaving or reloading the document removes the inspector.

Clipboard and retention

Copy buttons or the C shortcut build the requested output locally and write it to your clipboard. StyleSnap never reads your clipboard. Clipboard output and anything you paste remain under your control after the inspector closes or the extension is uninstalled.

StyleSnap has no persistent data storage: it uses no cookies, local storage, databases or extension storage. Inspection state is held in document memory. Closing the panel removes its UI and interaction handlers; document-side script state can remain until the document unloads. Your browser (Chrome or Firefox), not StyleSnap, manages extension settings and assigned keyboard shortcuts. In Chrome you can change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts; in Firefox, in about:addons under the gear menu’s Manage Extension Shortcuts.

Permissions and network activity

StyleSnap requests only activeTab, scripting and clipboardWrite: temporary selected-tab access, injection of its packaged inspector and writing requested clipboard output. It requests no host permissions or storage permission. Both editions request the same three permissions. In Firefox, the extension briefly checks the address of the tab you clicked, in memory only, so it can skip pages Firefox protects (such as about: pages and addons.mozilla.org) and show a “This page can’t be inspected” message; the address is not stored or sent.

The extension makes no network requests, uploads no inspected data and includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or remotely loaded executable code. In Firefox, StyleSnap declares to the browser that it collects and transmits no data (data collection permission “none”). Normal website activity and browser-managed services remain subject to their own policies.

Developer contact and your rights

I do not receive the data processed locally by the extension. If you email me, I receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer and handle your request, retaining it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Do not send extracted data unless it is needed for your request and you are entitled to share it. I do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

For personal data I hold, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable by contacting matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or your local authority.

Children and changes

This is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 16. Changes to this policy will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.
