PrintTidy privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 9 November 2026
Applies to: PrintTidy – Clean Print & PDF 0.2.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. PrintTidy processes articles locally in your browser. It has no account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud upload or sync. It does not collect, upload, sell or share your data.

What is read. Only when you click the PrintTidy toolbar button, the add-on reads the page in your current tab, copies it in memory and uses the bundled Mozilla Readability library to find the article. It never fetches articles from a server.

What is stored. - Article HTML, title, URL, byline and site name, with the original tab’s ID and an expiry time, in browser session storage on your device — expires after 15 minutes. Timers and background startup remove expired entries; Firefox clears session storage when it restarts. An open preview tab keeps its copy in memory until you close it. - Named presets (layout choices plus header and footer text, never article text) in browser local storage until you delete them in settings or remove PrintTidy. - HTML or Markdown exports go wherever you choose to save them. PrintTidy uses no sync storage. Usage counters left by earlier versions are deleted when the add-on starts.

Network. The add-on itself sends no network requests to the publisher or any third party (extension pages use CSP connect-src 'none'). The preview page loads nothing from the web; its images are data: URLs made from pixels the page had already loaded and that the browser allows it to read; other images are left out. The original page’s own images and links can still cause traffic to those sites. Whole-page cleanup prints the original website tab, which keeps its normal network activity. Printing and Save as PDF use the browser’s own print dialog — your printer or PDF destination, and links you follow in exported files, may contact other services independently of PrintTidy.

Permissions. - activeTab: temporary access to the current tab after you click the toolbar button; - scripting: runs article extraction (bundled content.js) and, if you choose it, whole-page cleanup in that tab; - storage: temporary preview in session storage and presets in local storage. No host permissions. No declared content scripts (content.js is injected only after the toolbar click). Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"].

Deletion. Delete one preset or all presets in PrintTidy settings. Close a preview tab to discard its copy; restarting Firefox clears session storage. Removing PrintTidy deletes the data it stored.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
