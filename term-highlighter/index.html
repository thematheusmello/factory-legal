TermGlow – Multi-Keyword Highlighter

TermGlow is a free Firefox desktop add-on that highlights many words and phrases at once, each list in its own colour, so you can scan long pages for what matters.

  • Save as many term lists as you like. Each list has one of eight colours and its own whole-word and match-case options.
  • Highlight the current page from the toolbar button, or press Ctrl+Shift+7 (changeable in Firefox’s add-on shortcut settings).
  • See how often each term appears, list by list, and jump from one match to the next. Highlighting keeps up as new content appears; clearing puts the page’s text back exactly as it was.
  • Optional automatic highlighting for every website, with a list of sites where TermGlow should never run.
  • Import and export lists as JSON (all options) or CSV (list, term, color). TermGlow checks the whole file before it replaces anything, and you can undo an import.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no affiliate links and no payments. Nothing you type is touched: no highlights in form fields, editable areas, or scripts and styles the page runs.

Privacy. Your lists and settings stay in this browser’s local extension storage and are never sent anywhere. Page text is matched on your device. TermGlow makes no network requests of its own; the websites you visit still load their own content as usual. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Highlights the main page, not content inside embedded frames or shadow-DOM components. Firefox does not let add-ons run on its own pages (such as about: pages) or on add-on stores. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale claim.
