Postmark Rambles privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Postmark Rambles is an HTML5 word-search game published by Matheus da Silveira Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal (contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com). The game is played on third-party game platforms (“platforms”), such as CrazyGames and YouTube Playables, and as a local build.

Summary

  • The game does not ask for a name, email, birthday or game account and does not assign a tracking identifier. There are no purchases.
  • The game has no analytics, game server or advertising requests. Ads are disabled.
  • Your progress is saved on your device or through the platform’s save feature.
  • Sharing a challenge is voluntary; the link contains only a format version, numeric seed, route, word language, completion time and hint count.

Save data

To let you continue, the game saves a small versioned JSON record: an earned-star score; destination stamps with best stars, completion times and hint counts; the latest daily-trail seed and result; music and sound-effect on/off and volume settings; and any unfinished grid (its seed, route, mode, word language, found words, elapsed time, hints used and highlighted hint cell). The local build uses browser localStorage under atlas-words. The CrazyGames build uses its SDK Data module with the same key. The YouTube Playables build uses host SDK progress storage and submits an integer earned-star score; outside the actual Playables host, its adapter uses local browser storage. The save contains no name, contact details or account identifier and is not sent to the publisher.

Browser-local saves remain until you or your browser clear the game’s site data. Clear site storage in your browser settings to remove the local atlas, unfinished grid and results. Platform saves follow the platform’s storage and account controls. The publisher runs no separate progress database and has no server copy to delete. The game cannot access or delete the platform’s account data; contact the platform for those controls. Failed loads and corrupt or unsupported future saves are protected from automatic overwrite.

Challenges

On local and CrazyGames builds, an explicit share-a-challenge action after a trail shows a link containing only a format version, numeric seed, route, word language, completion time and hint count. Sharing is voluntary and performed by you. No name or account ID is included. Anyone with the link can read these values and replay the same grid; results are self-reported and are not authenticated. There is no public leaderboard, chat or automatic publication. The game never reads the clipboard. It writes the link only after you press Copy link; if copying is denied or unavailable, the link remains as selectable text. The YouTube Playables build contains no clipboard calls or in-game sharing controls and displays a plain trail record only.

A hosting service may receive challenge URL query parameters in its ordinary request logs; the game adds no logging service.

The daily trail derives its seed locally from your device’s UTC calendar date. No location or time service is contacted.

Platforms and ads

Only the selected portal SDK is loaded remotely; local play uses bundled resources only. All game assets and fonts are bundled, and art and audio are generated locally. The game has no external API, analytics, tracker or score server. It makes no advertising requests and its ad integration is disabled. The optional extra-hint reward cannot request a live portal ad in this configuration; unavailable or cancelled rewards do not grant extra hints. The YouTube Playables build may tell the host that an error or warning occurred, with no details attached. Platforms and browsers may independently process account, device, connection and platform-storage information under their own privacy policies — for example CrazyGames and Google/YouTube. The game does not receive account credentials or read personal host data. This policy does not describe or override the platforms’ independent processing.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, push notifications, file-system, sign-in or payment permissions. Web Audio synthesizes music and sound effects locally after interaction, respecting your audio preferences and host audio restrictions. Clipboard writing is limited to the player-initiated Copy link action described above.

Children

Postmark Rambles is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

If you email me, I use your email only to answer you and keep the conversation only as long as needed. You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data I hold by writing to matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or the authority in your country.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
