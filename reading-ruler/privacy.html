LineGuide privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 9 November 2026
Applies to: LineGuide – Reading Ruler & Line Focus 0.2.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. LineGuide is a free reading ruler. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry or affiliate links, and the publisher runs no server for it. Your settings stay in your browser. The extension contacts a website itself only when you ask it to open a PDF from a web address, as described below. The websites you visit make their own network requests as usual; LineGuide does not make browsing network-free.

What is stored. Stored only in this browser’s extension storage (storage.local), never synced or sent anywhere by LineGuide: your reading settings (style, colour, ruler height, highlight strength, mask darkness and whether keyboard mode is on); sites you ask it to start on automatically, saved as scheme and host name only (for example https://example.com); and up to five named presets of those settings. Nothing else is stored: no page text, browsing history, PDF content or reading activity.

What LineGuide reads while you use it. Pointer position and the ↑/↓ keys, to move the ruler on the page you are reading — nothing is recorded. The background colour under the ruler, to choose how the ruler blends with the page so the line you are reading stays legible; this happens in the page and is not stored. Whether the ruler is showing on the current tab, checked when you open the toolbar popup so its switch shows On or Off; the check returns only true or false and does not change the page. The current tab’s address, when you open the popup, to offer “Start automatically” for that site or “Open this PDF in the reader” on a PDF address. The ruler is a visual layer drawn over the page (inside its own shadow root). It does not rewrite, collect or transmit the page’s text.

Network. Website traffic belongs to the website. While LineGuide is installed, pages you visit load and communicate exactly as they would without it. LineGuide itself makes no network requests, with one exception that you start: “Open this PDF in the reader”. When you choose it, your browser first asks you to allow LineGuide access to that site. LineGuide then downloads that one PDF address directly from its website, with no cookies or stored credentials, no referrer, and redirects refused. The website receives the request like any download: your IP address, the full address (including any query string) and ordinary connection details. Neither the publisher nor any intermediary receives the address or the document. The address is removed from the reader tab before the download, so reloading or restoring the tab does not fetch it again. If the download fails, or the PDF needs a login, download it with your browser and open the file in the reader instead. PDFs you choose or drop from your computer are read in the browser and never uploaded. The PDF engine (PDF.js) and all other code are bundled with the extension; no code, fonts or decoders are loaded from the internet. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. This declaration does not mean the extension never uses the network: the optional PDF download described above contacts the website you choose, at your request.

Permissions. storage saves the settings, sites and presets listed above. activeTab and scripting: when you use the toolbar popup or the keyboard shortcut, LineGuide may draw the ruler on the current tab, or check whether it is showing there. Nothing runs on a tab until you act on it. Optional site access is requested one site at a time, never at installation, when you turn on “Start automatically” for a site or open a PDF from a web address. Browsers cannot limit this access by port, so it covers that host on any port. Access you allow for one of these features also serves the other on the same site. Remove a site from LineGuide’s options page, or untick “Start automatically” in the popup, and LineGuide releases its access to that site. Access allowed only for opening a PDF stays until you remove it in your browser’s add-on permission settings. Withdrawing access in the browser pauses automatic start for that site until you allow it again.

Your choices and deleting data. Delete presets and sites on the options page. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored. When updating from an earlier edition, LineGuide keeps only settings, sites and presets and discards anything else it had stored. Your browser’s own history and downloads, and the server logs of websites you visit or download from, are governed by those parties.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months. What you send is handled separately from the extension.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
