LineGuide – Reading Ruler & Line Focus

LineGuide is a free Firefox desktop add-on that draws a reading ruler over the page you are reading and in its own PDF reader, so your eyes have one line to return to.

  • Three reading styles: a highlight bar, a focus mask that dims everything except your line, or a thin underline.
  • Eight named colours or any custom colour, plus adjustable ruler height, highlight strength and mask darkness. Changes show up straight away. The bar tints the page rather than washing over the words, on light pages and dark ones.
  • Keyboard mode moves the ruler with ↑ and ↓; form fields keep their own arrow keys. The default Firefox shortcut is Ctrl+Shift+7, which you can change in Firefox’s extension shortcut settings from LineGuide’s options page.
  • Start automatically on sites you choose. Your browser asks you to allow access to each site first.
  • Open a PDF from your computer in LineGuide’s reader, with selectable text and the ruler, or open a PDF address in the reader after you allow access to that site.
  • Save, apply and delete up to five presets of style, colour and size.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links and no payments.

Privacy. Settings, remembered sites (scheme and host only) and presets stay in local extension storage on your device, with no upload or account. The ruler is drawn over the page and never changes or collects its text. Websites you visit still make their own network requests. LineGuide itself contacts a website only when you choose “Open this PDF in the reader” for a PDF address and allow access to that site. PDFs from your computer are never uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale claim. Browsers do not let extensions draw on their own pages, add-on stores or built-in PDF viewers; use the PDF reader for PDFs. Encrypted and some unusual PDFs may not open. LineGuide is a reading aid, not a medical treatment.
