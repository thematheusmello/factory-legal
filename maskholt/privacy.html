Maskholt privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 13 November 2026
Applies to: Maskholt 1.0.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 128 or later).

Summary. Maskholt does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry, advertising, remote catalogue, remote preset updates or affiliate links. It makes no network requests of its own and contains no remote code. Presets, custom identity fields, site and every-site assignments and the last scope stay on your device.

What is stored. In the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) on your device: presets, custom identity fields, registrable-site assignments, the every-site choice and the last scope. This data stays on the device and is not synced by Maskholt. Tab-only choices use session storage and expire when the tab closes, leaves the chosen site, or the browser session ends. Local storage is not encrypted: exported files can contain website names and custom text, and you should protect any exported files yourself. This data is not sent to the publisher or to anyone else.

Identities and authorized sites. The extension uses the website access you grant to change the User-Agent request header and selected legacy navigator fields for the scopes you choose. Websites you visit then receive the identity you chose as part of ordinary browsing, under their own policies. Those requests belong to your browsing, not to an extension data service. Host access is optional and is requested when you apply a this-site or every-site scope. You may revoke it in Firefox’s extension permissions. “Restore all & remove site access” clears assignments and optional grants; presets remain until you delete them or uninstall Maskholt.

Network. Maskholt makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. Its own pages block network connections through a content security policy (connect-src 'none'). The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual. When an identity is in force, Firefox’s declarative request engine applies the chosen User-Agent to authorized traffic, and a MAIN-world script override updates the legacy navigator fields that page scripts can see. Maskholt does not add a proxy, change your IP address, or send browsing history, telemetry or usage metrics anywhere. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. Export writes a JSON file only when you choose Export. Importing a file reads it on the device. Legal and support links open only when you click them; if you open one in your browser, the hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request records; no extension or identity data is attached.

Permissions. - storage: saved identities, site and every-site assignments, and the last scope; session tab bindings; - declarativeNetRequest: set the HTTP User-Agent on authorized sites; - scripting: register a MAIN-world override so page scripts see the chosen navigator fields; - activeTab: identify the current page when you open the toolbar picker; - optional website access: requested when you apply a this-site or every-site scope. No website access is granted on a fresh installation.

Deletion. Delete identities in the library, use “Restore all & remove site access” to clear assignments and grants, or uninstall Maskholt, to remove extension data. Remove exported files wherever you saved them.

Support emails. Emailing support shares what you send with the publisher and email providers. No support message is sent automatically. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights. Where GDPR applies, you may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights, and complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Maskholt does not send extension data to the publisher, so most requests concern only messages you choose to send by email.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
