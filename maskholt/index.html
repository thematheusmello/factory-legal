Maskholt

Maskholt is a free Firefox desktop add-on that lets you pick a browser or device identity for a site, a tab, or every site. It changes both the User-Agent sent to the website and the legacy navigator values page scripts can see. Everything stays on your device.

  • Keep a choice for a site, including its subdomains, after quitting and reopening Firefox. Choose an every-site identity after a clear confirmation, and keep it after a restart.
  • Try an identity in this tab on this site. A this-tab-only choice ends when the tab closes or leaves the site, and does not survive a restart.
  • Restore the selected scope in one click. Restoring every site clears all assignments. The library asks before deleting an identity or restoring everything.
  • Edit, delete, or add identities in a small, dated library. Export and import your saved choices as local Maskholt JSON. A short toolbar badge shows when a different identity is in force.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links and no payments.

Privacy. Presets, custom identity fields, site and every-site assignments and the last scope stay in local extension storage and are never synced. Tab-only choices use session storage and expire when the tab closes, leaves the site, or the browser session ends. Maskholt itself makes no network requests and uses connect-src 'none'. When an identity is in force, websites you visit receive the User-Agent you chose as part of ordinary browsing, and page scripts can see the chosen legacy navigator fields. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 128 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale support is claimed in this Firefox listing. This is not device emulation, a proxy, or fingerprint protection. Chromium Client Hints and navigator.userAgentData are outside this Firefox kit. Browser-protected pages and workers are not covered. Page scripts can detect or change navigator overrides. A site that rewrites window.name can interrupt a tab-only choice; reapply it or use a site choice. Website access is requested when you apply a scope. A fresh install changes nothing.
