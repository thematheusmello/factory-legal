These terms cover both the Chrome and Firefox editions of GridGrab 0.1.0. Both are free and require no account. They extract tables and repeated lists from the selected page, with unlimited TSV copying for Excel or Sheets and a combined limit of five CSV, XLSX, Markdown or JSON exports per UTC day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. CSV/XLSX are downloads; TSV/Markdown/JSON are clipboard output. Failed output can still count in Chrome; Firefox attempts to restore the count when output throws an error.

Firefox disables private browsing and removes highlights and candidate references when the popup connection closes.
