Sheetkeel – Table & List to CSV/Excel

Sheetkeel was previously called GridGrab.

Updated 15 November 2026

Sheetkeel is a Chrome and Firefox extension that turns tables and repeated lists on the web page you are viewing into spreadsheet data. Preview the rows, pick and rename columns, then copy TSV for Excel or Google Sheets, download CSV or XLSX, or copy Markdown or JSON. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

Sheetkeel is free, requires no account and has no Pro plan. TSV, CSV, XLSX, Markdown and JSON exports are all unlimited, with no daily export cap.
