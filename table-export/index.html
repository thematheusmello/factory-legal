GridGrab – Table & List to CSV/Excel

GridGrab is a Chrome and Firefox extension that turns tables and repeated lists on the web page you are viewing into spreadsheet data. Preview the rows, pick and rename columns, then copy TSV for Excel or Google Sheets, download CSV or XLSX, or copy Markdown or JSON. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

GridGrab is free and requires no account. TSV copying is unlimited. CSV, XLSX, Markdown and JSON share a combined limit of five exports per UTC day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Failed output can still count toward the daily limit.
