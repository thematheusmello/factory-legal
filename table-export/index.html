GridGrab – Table & List to CSV/Excel

GridGrab is a Chrome extension that turns tables and repeated lists on the web page you are viewing (search results, directories, product grids) into clean spreadsheet data. Preview the rows, pick and rename columns, then copy to Excel or Google Sheets or download CSV/XLSX, Markdown or JSON. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Free: unlimited clipboard copy and 5 file exports per day. Pro ($24 once or $3.99/month) adds unlimited exports and multi-page capture.
