Origami Canal

Origami Canal is a one-tap skill game about a little paper boat on a never-ending canal. Tap to switch direction at every bend, ride the current, and keep off the sandy banks. Stone lock gates open and close on a rhythm, so plan your line to slip through while they’re open. Collect lotus flowers along the way to fold new boats: a crane, a frog, a swan, a dragon and more. The canal narrows and speeds up the farther you sail; runs are quick, retries are instant, and your best score and boats are saved. Free to play on supported game platforms (for example CrazyGames and YouTube Playables), with ads provided by the platform.
