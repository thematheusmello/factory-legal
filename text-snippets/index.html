QuickType – Text Snippet Expander

QuickType is a free text expander extension for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

QuickType turns a short shortcut such as ;sig into your saved text, right where you type: web inputs, textareas and rich-text editors.

  • Type a shortcut and it expands in place, or press Alt+Shift+Space to search your snippets.
  • Placeholders: {date}, {time}, {cursor}, {input:Name} fill-ins and {clipboard}. The clipboard placeholder needs a separate, optional permission.
  • Folders, search, JSON/CSV import and JSON export.
  • Works only on the sites you enable from the toolbar button, or on all sites if you choose.
  • Never reads or expands password, one-time-code or payment card fields.
  • Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Privacy. Your snippets stay in this browser unless you export them or turn on sync. Sync is off by default. When you turn it on, QuickType copies your whole library to Firefox sync storage. If you sync add-ons with a Mozilla account, Mozilla stores the library and sends it to your other devices. Firefox asks for your consent first, because snippets often contain names, addresses and message text. QuickType's publisher never receives your snippets, and the add-on makes no network requests of its own. Text you insert into a website can be read by that website.

Shortcuts work after you reload a newly enabled page. Protected Firefox pages and canvas-based editors are not supported. You can change the Alt+Shift+Space shortcut in Firefox's add-on shortcut settings.
