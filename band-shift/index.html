Band Shift

Band Shift is a free Google Sheets editor add-on that shades consecutive row groups: the fill color switches whenever a key value changes.

  • Select one range, open Extensions → Band Shift → Open, and choose the key column, whether the first row is a header, gray/blue/green with white or two custom #RRGGBB colors, exact or case/space-insensitive matching, and how blank key cells are grouped.
  • Preview shows how many rows and groups the selection has and where the first groups start, without changing anything.
  • Apply changes fill colors only; values, formulas, borders and number formats are not touched.
  • Undo last change restores the fill colors from before the last Apply or Clear for that exact range. Clear fill colors names the exact range and asks for confirmation.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no tracking. Works on one range of up to 50,000 cells at a time.

Privacy. The add-on works only in the open Sheet. It requests spreadsheets.currentonly and script.container.ui only. The Undo snapshot (sheet ID, A1 range, earlier fill colors — never cell values) is stored in the spreadsheet’s document properties. No network calls and no telemetry. See the privacy policy.
