Feltfall Encore

Effective date: 8 November 2026

A little fox in a felt mask tiptoes through a cardboard theatre whose scenery plays tricks.

An HTML5 puzzle platformer with 40 handmade stages across five acts: 20 original sets and 20 Encores. Checkpoints, an optional Assist mode and a hint ghost are all free. Separate music and sound controls, reduced motion and opt-in haptics are included.

Published by Matheus da Silveira Mello. Support: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com.
