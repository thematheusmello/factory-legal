CiteLocal privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 9 November 2026
Applies to: CiteLocal – Citation Generator APA MLA 0.2.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. CiteLocal is a free citation draft tool. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry or affiliate links, and the publisher runs no server for it. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. What it reads or saves stays in your browser unless you copy or export it yourself. The websites you visit make their own network requests as usual; CiteLocal does not make browsing network-free.

What CiteLocal reads, and when. Only when you click the CiteLocal toolbar button. Firefox then gives CiteLocal temporary access to that one tab (the activeTab permission), and CiteLocal runs its bundled metadata reader once in it. The reader looks at the page’s citation metadata: meta tags (citation, Dublin Core, OpenGraph and article tags), schema.org JSON-LD, the page title, the first heading and a byline, plus the page address. It records today’s date as the access date. It does not read the rest of the page text, form fields, passwords, cookies, browsing history or other tabs, and it does not change the page. It never runs in the background, and there are no keyboard shortcuts that could start it. Browser pages and protected sites refuse access, and CiteLocal tells you so.

What is stored. Nothing is saved until you choose Add to list. Then the source details, as you edited them, are saved in the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) together with your list names, the selected list and your preferred citation style. CiteLocal does not sync, upload, sell or share this. It is not encrypted, so anyone who uses your browser profile can see it. The citation preview itself exists only while the popup is open.

Network. CiteLocal itself makes no network requests: no DOI lookups, analytics, telemetry, advertising, affiliate tracking, payment code or remotely loaded code. Its pages run under a content security policy that blocks network connections. DOIs are treated as text only and are never looked up. Websites you visit still load over the network as usual, and Firefox and the add-on store may contact their own services, for example for updates, under their own policies. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. This declaration does not mean browsing is network-free.

Clipboard and export. When you copy, CiteLocal writes only the citation or reference list you asked for to the clipboard; it never reads the clipboard. When you export, your browser saves a file to your downloads. What happens to copied text and exported files is up to you, and the apps or people you share them with have their own practices.

Permissions. storage keeps your lists, entries and settings in this browser. activeTab gives temporary access to the current tab after you click the toolbar button. scripting runs the bundled metadata reader in that tab. CiteLocal requests no host permissions, registers no content scripts and defines no keyboard shortcuts.

Your choices and deleting data. Remove single entries or delete whole lists in Your lists. Uninstalling CiteLocal normally deletes its storage, so export anything you want to keep first. Deleting a list does not delete files you exported or text you copied.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months. What you send is handled separately from the extension.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the notice will be published at https://thematheusmello.github.io/factory-legal/cite-page/.
