CiteLocal – Citation Generator APA MLA

CiteLocal is a free Firefox desktop add-on that builds a citation from the web page you are reading. Click the toolbar button and it uses the page’s own citation metadata, ready to check, edit and copy.

  • Every style is free: APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago notes-bibliography and author-date, Harvard, IEEE and Vancouver, each with an in-text citation or first note.
  • Check and edit every field: title, authors (people or organisations), website or journal, publisher, dates, URL, source type and DOI. CiteLocal tells you when the page has no author, date or website name, and flags dates it cannot use.
  • Copy with italics for word processors, copy plain text, or copy just the in-text citation.
  • Save sources to as many local project lists as you like, limited only by browser storage.
  • Export a list as BibTeX, RIS or alphabetical text, or copy the whole reference list with hanging indents.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links and no payments.

Privacy. CiteLocal reads a page only when you click its button, keeps your lists in this browser, and makes no network requests of its own: no DOI lookups, no analytics, no ads, no affiliate links and no account. Websites you visit still load over the network as usual. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale claim. Browser pages and protected sites cannot be read, and CiteLocal says so. Website metadata can be missing or wrong, and these are web reference templates: journal volume, issue and page numbers, and the locators for notes, are not filled in for you. Harvard conventions vary between institutions, and numeric styles (IEEE, Vancouver) number your list in the order shown, so renumber to match your manuscript. Always check your official style guide. Style names describe citation formats; CiteLocal is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by their publishers.
