ReloadWatch – Auto Refresh & Change Alert

ReloadWatch is a free Firefox desktop add-on that reloads the tabs you choose and alerts you when text appears or disappears, or when an element you picked on the page changes.

  • Refresh every 10 seconds to 15 minutes, or any custom interval from 5 seconds.
  • Alert when text appears, when it disappears, or when a picked element changes. Pages that update without reloading are re-checked when they change.
  • Pick an element with a click (Esc cancels). Watch several tabs at once.
  • Save a rule for an exact URL or a URL prefix; it starts automatically when you open that page.
  • Optional random jitter, a reload limit, active hours (local time) and an alert tone.
  • Refresh pauses while you type in the page, and resumes 10 seconds after you leave the field.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

How it works. Checks run inside your browser. ReloadWatch sends nothing to us or any third party. Each reload is a normal visit to the watched website, with your usual cookies. Firefox asks for access to each site you watch; if you decline, nothing starts.

Limits. Timing is best effort. Intervals under 30 seconds follow a one-second page tick and can land up to about a second late. Browser sleep and background-tab throttling can delay reloads. Tested on Firefox on Linux only. See the privacy policy.
