2. Features and limits

Color Tally counts, sums and summarizes cells by fill or font color in the current spreadsheet. Tallies and inserted static values support Fill, Font and Fill + font matching, with exact matching or CIE76 ΔE tolerance from 0 to 20. All-color summaries and summary tables group exact Fill or Font colors only; they do not support tolerance or combined matching.

Each source operation is limited to 100,000 cells. Saved definitions have an approximately 8,000-byte capacity per spreadsheet. Conditional-formatting colors are not detected. Outputs are static values, not custom functions or formulas, and do not recalculate automatically. Use Refresh all when you want saved outputs recalculated.

3. Your spreadsheet and writes

Use the add-on only in spreadsheets you are authorized to access and modify. Review the confirmation and destination before each write, and keep appropriate backups. Inserting a value overwrites the destination value and note. Summary tables clear content and formatting in their five-column footprint; replacing a saved table may also clear its larger previous footprint.

Refresh all overwrites saved destinations, including manual edits, and clears table content and formatting before rewriting. It can partially succeed: other outputs may update even when one fails. Cell writes and saved metadata updates are not atomic or all-or-nothing. Confirmations do not guarantee recovery or prevent every conflicting edit.

Saved A1 references do not follow renamed sheets or moved ranges, and can become stale after structural edits such as inserted rows or columns. Verify sources and destinations again after those changes. Forget removes a saved definition and stops its refresh; it does not delete or restore output cells.
