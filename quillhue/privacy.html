Quillhue privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy covers the Quillhue browser extension (“Quillhue”), published by Matheus Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal. Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Quillhue collects no personal data and sends nothing to me or anyone else.
  • No analytics, advertising, affiliate links, remote configuration or remotely loaded code. The extension makes no network requests.
  • Quillhue is free. No account or purchase is required.

What the extension reads, and when

Quillhue does nothing on a page until you start it from its toolbar button or keyboard shortcut. Then, only for the current tab:

  • Picking a colour. In Chrome and Edge it uses the browser’s built-in eyedropper, which returns only the single colour you click. Where that eyedropper is not available (for example in Firefox), Quillhue takes a snapshot of the visible part of the page and shows a magnifier over it. The snapshot stays in memory in that tab and is discarded when picking ends. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Page colours. When you ask for them, Quillhue reads the computed colours of visible elements on the page. It does not read form entries.

What is stored on your device

Your picked-colour history, named palettes and preferences are saved in the browser’s extension storage on your device. They are not synced by the extension and are not sent to me. You can clear them in the extension, and they are removed when you uninstall it. Copying to the clipboard happens only when you choose a copy action.

Permissions

activeTab gives temporary access to the tab you started Quillhue on, including the visible-area snapshot used by the fallback picker. scripting runs the bundled picker and page-colour code in that tab. storage keeps the data described above. Quillhue has no standing access to any website.

Sharing, rights and changes

I receive no data from the extension, so nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. If you email me, I use your message only to reply, and you may ask me to delete it. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD). Changes to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
