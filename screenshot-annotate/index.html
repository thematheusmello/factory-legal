SnapRedact – Full-Page Screenshot & Blur

SnapRedact is a free Chrome extension for capturing a visible area, a selected area or a full scrolling page. Annotate screenshots with arrows, boxes, text and highlights, or edit them with blur, pixelation, solid cover and crop tools.

Optional Auto-blur scans page text and eligible form fields locally for email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers, suggesting blur boxes for you to review. Export PNG, JPG or PDF, or copy a PNG image to your clipboard. Very tall captures are split into images of up to 32,000 device pixels in height.

No account is required. The extension processes captures in your browser and makes no network requests. Its local gallery retains up to 100 captures, including page titles and capture times. Opening the editor saves the initial image with any auto-blur applied; later manual edits are saved to the gallery when you download or copy. See the privacy policy for original-image retention and deletion details.

Review every image before sharing. Auto-blur can miss sensitive content, including text inside images, and blur or pixelation can leave information recognisable. Use solid Cover or Crop for sensitive areas and inspect the exported result. Quick copy skips this review. Capture and stitching can be incomplete on dynamic pages; Chrome restricts capture on browser and store pages.
