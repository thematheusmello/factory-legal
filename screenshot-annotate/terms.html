Memory limits, browser restrictions, page changes and compatibility can limit operation or cause incomplete captures and lost data.

Auto-blur is a pattern-based helper and can miss sensitive information or mark harmless text. It does not recognise text inside images. Blur and pixelation may leave information recognisable. Use solid Cover or Crop for sensitive areas, inspect the final export, and avoid quick copy when review is needed. No redaction tool guarantees confidentiality.

Choose Agree and continue before capture or scanning. Not now leaves capture disabled. Withdraw consent in Settings to block new captures, pending handoffs and Copy or Download from existing editors; agreeing again does not reauthorize earlier captures. Export-format, Auto-blur and quick-copy preferences and the consent record persist locally.

Closing or reloading the editor discards its capture and edits; a background-context restart before handoff can lose a pending capture. Original pixels and undo history can remain in an open editor after editing or export; withdrawing consent does not erase them. Only explicit export actions export pixels: Copy, Download, or Chrome’s explicitly selected quick-copy action; Firefox quick-copy mode requires clicking Copy & close. Keep needed exports safely.
