ContactSift privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how the ContactSift – Email & Phone Extractor browser extension (“ContactSift”) handles information. ContactSift is published by Matheus Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal (“I”, “me”). Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com.

Scope and free use

This policy covers ContactSift for Chrome. It is free, works locally and requires no account or server. It does not upload contacts, page contents, source URLs or browsing history, and makes no extension-initiated network requests. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking or remotely loaded executable code. Normal website activity and browser-managed services remain subject to their own policies.

What the extension reads

When you click “Scan this page”, the bundled scanner reads the selected HTTP/HTTPS tab’s rendered body text, mailto/tel link values and Cloudflare-encoded email attributes. Link and encoded-attribute searches are not restricted to visible elements. It also reads the page title and source URL (origin and path, without query parameters or fragment) for source attribution. It recognises common email obfuscations, normalises and deduplicates email addresses, and checks and formats phone numbers locally using bundled numbering metadata and your selected default country. It does not verify that an address is deliverable or a number belongs to a particular person.

ContactSift does not crawl linked pages, automatically scan other tabs or send messages. Browser and store pages can block scanning. Scan results stay in popup memory until replaced by another scan or the popup closes; they are saved persistently only when you choose to add selected contacts.

Local storage and limits

Up to 100 contacts in total can be saved across local named collections, including the default “My collection”. Contacts are deduplicated within each collection; the same value in separate collections occupies separate slots. Saved records include contact type, email address or international phone number, national phone formatting, source page title and source URL (origin and path), context snippet and discovery timestamp. Treat these records as potentially personal or sensitive information.

chrome.storage.local stores these collections and their names, the default phone country, settings fields for filtering and collection selection, and the UTC daily scan date/count. No extension-managed sync is used. Settings allow named collections, a page-domain email filter and excluded-value filters. Five scans are allowed per UTC day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. A scan counts only after page access and scanner execution succeed, even if no contacts are found or filters hide all results. Unsupported pages and page-access failures do not consume scans. Copying, CSV export and saving existing scan results do not consume additional scans.

Clipboard, downloads and deletion

You can copy selected contact values as newline-separated text or download selected results as a dated CSV file. Settings also allow copying or exporting a saved collection and creating, renaming, deleting or clearing collections. The default collection can be cleared but not renamed or deleted; collection export filenames include the collection name. CSV contains type, value, national phone formatting, page title, source URL (origin and path) and discovery time; context snippets remain in saved records but are not included in CSV. Clipboard actions write values only and do not read your clipboard. Files are generated locally, and the browser may keep download history.

Saved records remain until you clear the collection, clear the extension’s local data or uninstall. Clearing the collection does not reset all preferences or the scan counter. Exported files and pasted or clipboard copies remain under your control and are not removed by clearing the extension or uninstalling it.

Permissions

ContactSift requests only activeTab, scripting and storage, with no website host permissions. These provide temporary access to the selected tab, run the bundled scanner and save the local records and preferences described above.

Developer contact and your rights

I do not receive the data processed locally by the extension. If you email me, I receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer and handle your request, retaining it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Do not send extracted data unless it is needed for your request and you are entitled to share it. I do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

For personal data I hold, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable by contacting matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or your local authority.
