ContactSift privacy policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the ContactSift – Email & Phone Extractor browser extension (“ContactSift”) handles information. ContactSift is published by Matheus Mello, an individual developer based in Portugal (“I”, “me”). Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com.

Summary

  • ContactSift works locally in your browser. I do not receive the contacts you extract, page contents, URLs or your browsing history.
  • No analytics, advertising, trackers or remotely loaded code.
  • No account is needed for free use.
  • Optional Pro purchases are handled by ExtensionPay and its payment processor Stripe.

What the extension reads

When you click “Scan this page”, ContactSift reads the visible text, email and phone links and Cloudflare-encoded email attributes of the tab you selected, to find publicly listed contacts. It does not crawl websites, read other tabs or send messages. Scan results stay in the popup’s memory until it closes.

What is stored on your device

The extension stores the contacts you choose to save (email address or phone number, source page URL, page title, a short context snippet and the time found), your named collections, default phone country, filter preferences, the daily scan counter and your licence status in chrome.storage.local on your device. This data is not synced by the extension and is not sent to me. You can delete it in the extension’s settings (or by clearing the extension’s data), and it is removed when you uninstall the extension. Files you export and text you copy stay wherever you put them and are under your control.

Optional Pro purchases

Pro features (unlimited scans and contacts, named collections, domain filter and exclude lists) are sold through ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe to process payments. When you choose to upgrade, or when the extension checks whether your Pro licence is active, it communicates with ExtensionPay. ExtensionPay and Stripe process the information needed for the purchase and licence — such as your email address, licence status, transaction details and billing information — under their own privacy policies (ExtensionPay, Stripe). Card details are entered on the provider’s checkout page; the extension never sees them. No extracted page data is sent to them.

If you buy Pro, I receive from ExtensionPay/Stripe your email address, what you bought, the amount, your country and the payment status. I use this only to provide and support your licence, handle refunds and complaints, and meet accounting and tax obligations (legal bases: performance of a contract and legal obligation, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)). I keep purchase records for as long as Portuguese tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 10 years) and other support emails for as long as needed to handle your request.

Sharing

I do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for advertising. Purchase data is processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe as described above, and may be disclosed to authorities where the law requires it. Some providers may process data outside the EEA; they rely on safeguards recognised by EU law, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Permissions

ContactSift requests activeTab, scripting and storage only. activeTab gives temporary access to the tab you click the extension on; scripting runs the bundled code that reads that page; storage keeps the local data described above. It has no standing access to all websites. When Pro payments are enabled, the extension also includes ExtensionPay’s small script that runs only on extensionpay.com pages to confirm a purchase.

Your rights

You can ask me for access to, correction or deletion of personal data I hold about you, restrict or object to processing, or ask for data portability, by writing to matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com. For data held by ExtensionPay or Stripe you can also contact them directly. You may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or the authority in your country.

Children

ContactSift is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the extension’s store listing.
