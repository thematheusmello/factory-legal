3. Free features and limits

ContactSift is free and requires no account. Before scanning, you must review the in-product disclosure and choose “I agree”; “Decline” keeps scanning off. Agreeing alone does not read a page. After consent, clicking “Scan this page” scans the selected HTTP/HTTPS page for email addresses and phone numbers, handles common email obfuscations and encoded email attributes, and checks and formats phone numbers locally using a selected default country. It supports selecting results, copying values, CSV export, deduplication and saving contacts in named local collections, with a total limit of 100 saved contacts across all collections. Contacts are deduplicated within each collection. Settings support page-domain and excluded-value filters, and creating, renaming, deleting, copying, exporting or clearing collections; the default “My collection” cannot be renamed or deleted.

Five scans are allowed per UTC day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Scans count only after page access and scanner execution succeed, including scans with no contacts or with all results filtered out. Unsupported pages and page-access failures do not consume scans. Copying, exporting and saving current results do not consume additional scans. Scanning uses temporary selected-tab access, with no standing website host permissions, and may be blocked on browser or store pages. See the privacy policy for saved fields, local storage, source URLs, clipboard and download handling.

Settings shows the disclosure and consent status. “Withdraw consent” removes the consent record, disables further scanning even in an open popup and clears unsaved results. Saved contacts and collections remain until you delete or clear them. Clipboard copies and exported files remain under your control. To scan again, review the disclosure and choose “I agree” in the popup.
