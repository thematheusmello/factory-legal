ContactSift – Email & Phone Extractor

ContactSift is a Chrome extension that finds publicly listed email addresses and phone numbers on the web page you are viewing, validates phone numbers per country, and lets you keep a deduplicated contact collection that you can export as CSV. It reads one page at a time, only when you click “Scan this page”, and everything stays in your browser. Free: 5 scans per day and up to 100 saved contacts. Pro ($29 once or $4.99/month) adds unlimited scans and contacts, named collections, a page-domain filter and exclude lists.
