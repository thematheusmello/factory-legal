ContactSift – Email & Phone Extractor

ContactSift is a Chrome extension that finds email addresses and phone numbers on the web page you are viewing when you click “Scan this page”. It checks and formats phone numbers locally using your selected default country; it does not verify deliverability or ownership. You can select results, copy contact values, export CSV and save contacts locally in named collections, with a total limit of 100 saved contacts.

ContactSift is free and requires no account. It allows five scans per UTC day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Scans count after page access and scanner execution succeed; unsupported pages and page-access failures do not consume a scan. Copying, exporting and saving current results do not consume additional scans. Processing and saved contacts stay in your browser.
