Gaugemere privacy policy

Publisher: Matheus da Silveira Mello
Contact: matheus.mello.developer@gmail.com
Effective date: 13 November 2026
Applies to: Gaugemere 0.1.0 for Firefox desktop (Firefox 142 or later).

Summary. Gaugemere does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, payments, ads, analytics, telemetry or affiliate links. It makes no network requests and contains no remote code. Presets, settings and measurements stay on your device.

What is stored. In the browser’s extension storage (storage.local) on your device: custom preset names, dimensions and optional window positions; preferences such as default resize mode, the optional page badge and favorites; last-used size; and the last measured display work area. This data is not sent to the publisher or to anyone else, and Gaugemere does not sync it between devices.

Page measurement and badge. When you invoke Gaugemere on a regular webpage, it reads window and viewport dimensions and available screen bounds. It can display a temporary size badge on the page. It does not read page text, form values or browsing history. Restricted browser pages cannot be measured or receive the badge. Window resizing remains available.

Network. Gaugemere makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. Its own pages block network connections through a content security policy (connect-src 'none'). The websites you visit still make their own network requests as usual. Gaugemere does not add to, read or change that traffic. Firefox data collection declaration: required ["none"]. Import reads only a JSON file you choose and never fetches URLs from a file. Export writes custom presets to a file you choose to keep. If you open a legal or support link in your browser, the hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request records; no extension or preset data is attached.

Permissions. - activeTab: after you click the toolbar, use a command or choose the context menu, measure the current page and optionally show the brief badge. No persistent host access; - scripting: run only the bundled dimension reader and badge helpers on that tab; - storage: save presets and preferences on your device; - contextMenus: add the Gaugemere page menu (last used, rotate and two desktop favorites). No host permissions and no automatic content scripts are declared at install.

Deletion. You can edit or delete custom presets in settings, and remove exported files wherever you saved them. Uninstalling Gaugemere deletes everything it stored in the browser.

Support emails. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you, keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it within 12 months.

Your rights. Where GDPR applies, you may exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights, and complain to Portugal’s Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). Gaugemere does not send extension data to the publisher, so most requests concern only messages you choose to send by email.

Changes. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
