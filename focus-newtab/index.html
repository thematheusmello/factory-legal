Stillpoint – New Tab Tasks & Focus

Stillpoint is a free Firefox desktop add-on that replaces your new-tab page with a clock, your main focus for the day, task lists and a focus/break timer.

  • Add as many tasks and lists as your browser storage allows. Tick them off, drag them into order, or press Alt + ↑/↓ on a task to move it. Unfinished tasks carry into the next day automatically.
  • Start, pause and reset focus or break sessions; browser alarms keep time and a notification tells you when a session ends.
  • Choose light, dark or match-device appearance, six backgrounds (each with a light and dark version), a solid colour of your own, or a local photo.
  • See completed focus sessions for the last 30 days. Keep seven daily task snapshots, restore one in a click, or export and import a JSON backup.

Every feature is free. No account, no ads, no analytics, no affiliate links and no payments.

Privacy. Tasks, lists, timer, sessions, settings, custom backgrounds and snapshots stay in local extension storage on your device, with no upload or account. Stillpoint’s own code makes no remote fetches (CSP connect-src 'none'). The optional search box uses Firefox’s browser.search.query, so queries go through your default search engine as ordinary browser-mediated traffic. See the privacy policy.

Limits. Desktop Firefox 142 or later only. Not for Firefox for Android. No Chrome, Edge, Opera or Whale claim. When you first open a new tab, Firefox asks whether to keep Stillpoint’s page — choose Keep Changes. Images ≤ 10 MB and backups ≤ 20 MB; timers may be late if the computer sleeps. Snapshots cover up to seven days and are not a cloud backup. No registered extension keyboard shortcuts.
